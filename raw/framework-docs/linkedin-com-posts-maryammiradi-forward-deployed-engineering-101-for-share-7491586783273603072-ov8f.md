🛠️🧭 Forward Deployed Engineering 101 for Production AI Agents.
Customers don't care about your agent architecture, but their business problem. 𝗠𝘆 𝟳-𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝗿𝗼𝗮𝗱𝗺𝗮𝗽 𝗳𝗿𝗼𝗺 𝗮 𝗰𝘂𝘀𝘁𝗼𝗺𝗲𝗿 𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗯𝗹𝗲𝗺 𝘁𝗼 𝗮 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗱𝘂𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗔𝗜 𝗔𝗴𝗲𝗻𝘁.

The core of Forward Deployed Engineering:
- Sell the outcome.
- Put engineering close to the customer.
- Build on shared primitives instead of starting from zero.

With AI agents, this matters even more.

The platform is highly technical.
The customer problem is highly specific.
The buyer often has no reason to understand the architecture underneath.

Here is how I would apply FDE to Production AI Agents:

》𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟭: Check If You Need FDE
✸ Use FDE when the product is highly technical.
✸ The buyer usually is not.
✸ Do not adopt FDE just because it is popular.
→ Example: An operations leader wants faster incident resolution, not a lesson in agent orchestration.

》𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟮: Build the Platform First
✸ Create reusable agent primitives.
✸ Tools, data access, auth, memory, evals, observability, human approval.
✸ Do not start every customer from a blank repo.
→ Otherwise you are building a dev shop, not an FDE function.

》𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟯: Put Engineers With Customers
✸ The FDE is still a software engineer.
✸ But they discover workflows, constraints, and exceptions directly.
✸ They translate business context into a working system.
→ Think “customer-facing software engineer.”

》𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟰: Configure, Do Not Rebuild
✸ Reuse as much of the platform as possible.
✸ Customize only what is truly customer-specific.
✸ Keep bespoke logic isolated.
→ Same agent runtime. Different tools, rules, data, and approval paths.

》𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟱: Build for the Outcome
✸ Start from the business result.
✸ Then design the agent workflow backwards.
✸ Do not sell architecture as the value.
→ The customer buys faster claims, safer operations, or fewer manual steps.

》𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟲: Generalize What Repeats
✸ Customer-specific needs stay customer-specific.
✸ Repeated patterns move into the platform.
✸ FDE becomes a discovery engine for the product team.
→ If the same approval pattern appears across customers, make it reusable.

》𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟳: Close the Production Loop
✸ Evaluate the real workflow.
✸ Log decisions and tool calls.
✸ Keep human gates for high-risk actions.
✸ Feed reusable learnings back into the platform.
→ Every deployment should make the next deployment easier.

𝗣𝗹𝗲𝗮𝘀𝗲 𝗥𝗲𝗺𝗲𝗺𝗯𝗲𝗿:
Forward Deployed Engineering is not custom consulting.
It turns a technical platform into customer outcomes.
For Production AI Agents:
shared primitives + customer context + customer-facing engineering + reusable learning.

It is important to understand the difference between the scientific method and the engineering method. Engineers do care about outcomes, but also the means (i.e. the methods) used to create them. Engineering methodology is used to design, build, operate, and regulate engineered systems connected to mission- and safety-sensitive applications. Just because we are now using AI technology doesn't mean we throw away engineering methods. In fact, due to inherent uncertainties within AI architectures, we need them more than ever.
