Stop over organizing Claude. It slows you down.
Here are 20 things to delete (& the 6 to keep):
Delete these today:
1. Your prompt library. You never open it.
2. The 40 skills you used once.
3. Every connector you added "just in case."
4. The 31-page prompting guide (I deleted mine).
5. The hacks you screenshot and never run.
6. Screenshots you collect instead of using Claude.
7. The 2nd subscription you forgot you're paying for.
8. The folders inside folders inside folders.
9. The "perfect setup" you still haven't started.
10. The chat history you'll "go back to read."
11. The YouTube tutorial saved at 3% watched.
12. The custom style you built & never switched on.
13. The project you created, used once, abandoned.
14. Global instructions so long Claude ignores half.
15. Thinking on "high" to ask about the weather.
16. The 6 connectors when you only use Gmail.
17. Skills that overlap with skills.
18. The organized Cowork folder & never opened.
19. Screenshots of other people's prompts.
20. The plugin pack you installed for one task.
Now the only 6 things to keep:
1. One "about-me" file.
One .md file: your role, tone, non-negotiables.
Claude reads it once & stops sounding like Claude. 
This single file beats every saved prompt template.
2. Give Claude the goal, not the steps.
"Write the email in 4 short paragraphs, friendly, no bullet points" = you doing its job. 
Say "reply to this so they say yes" and let it think. 
The more you micromanage, the dumber the output
3. Make it ask YOU questions first.
Add 1 line: "Before you answer, AskUserQuestion." 
Now it interviews you instead of guessing. 
You don't know how to prompt. It does. 
Let it pull the context out of you.
4. Never trust the first answer.
Reply with: "What's wrong with this? Now fix it." 
Claude critiquing Claude beats Claude on the first try. The first draft is a draft, not the answer.
5. Start a fresh chat every time.
A 40-message chat is dragging 39 old messages into every reply - that's why it gets slow, forgetful, & generic. New task = new chat. It's much faster.
6. Hand it the hardest task you have.
Most people give Claude the easy 5-min jobs & do the hard work themselves. Backwards. 
Give it the messy spreadsheet, the dense report, the thing you've been avoiding. That's where it saves you an afternoon, not 30 seconds.
That's the whole setup. One file. 6 habits. 0 folders.
Want the exact "about-me" file I use? 
It's free here → [how-to-ai.guide](https://www.linkedin.com/redir/redirect?url=http%3A%2F%2Fhow-to-ai%2Eguide&urlhash=6cp_&trk=public_post-text).
Reply to the welcome email. Don't pay anything.
Open the Notion link in the second email.
Go to the ".md file" folder and download the file.
♻️ Repost this so your network deletes the clutter and finally uses Claude.
