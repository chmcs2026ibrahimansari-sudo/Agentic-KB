# Garry Tan

Garry Tan is a prominent figure in the tech and startup ecosystem (president of Y Combinator) and the creator of **[GBrain](../frameworks/gbrain.md)**, an open-source personal AI agent framework.

## Relevance to This KB

Tan is notable here primarily as the creator and maintainer of GBrain. He has released the project under the MIT License and publicly frames it as a democratisation effort:

> "GBrain is my gift to you so you can have the same personal AI that I do. It's experimental but getting better every day."
