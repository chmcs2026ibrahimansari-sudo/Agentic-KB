# Databricks Workspace Platform Role — Strategy & Vision Notes

Personal capture of the role scope/expectations for a technical leadership position on Databricks' Workspace Platform team. Useful as prep material for interviews, negotiation, or onboarding framing.

## Core Responsibilities

- **Strategy & Vision**: Define and drive technical strategy for the Workspace Platform — described as owning **16+ shared capabilities** that underpin the entire Databricks experience.
- **Execution Ownership**: Own roadmap, execution, and delivery for the platform; accountable for team deliverables meeting quality and timeliness bars.
- **Engineering Excellence**: Establish/enforce best practices — design reviews, code quality, testing strategy, performance optimization — specifically for **high-volume distributed systems**.
- **Cross-Functional Collaboration**: Partner with teams building vertical product experiences — Lakehouse Platform, SQL Warehouse, AI/BI Dashboards, UC (Unity Catalog) Platform — plus core infrastructure teams, to ensure integration and innovation across the stack.

## Why It Matters (personal read)

This is a platform-leadership role, not a single-product IC/EM role — the "16 shared capabilities" framing signals a horizontal, foundational scope that touches most vertical product teams downstream. Engineering excellence ownership (design reviews, testing, perf) suggests this role sets standards org-wide, not just for one squad.
