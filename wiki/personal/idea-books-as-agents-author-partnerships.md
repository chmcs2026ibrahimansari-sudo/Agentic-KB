# Idea: Books as Apps/Agents — Author Partnership Model

## Concept

Turn any book into an interactive product: a website, app, or AI assistant/copilot/agent built around the book's content. The pitch is to reach out directly to authors and offer to build the experience for them in exchange for a **40% ownership stake**.

Core features envisioned for a "book agent":
- Chat with the book (conversational Q&A grounded in the text)
- Interactive games based on the book's content or themes
- AI-generated videos for specific chapters (using tools like LTX for video generation)
- Chapter-level summaries
- Personalized implementation guides — helping readers apply the book's ideas to their own life, plans, or goals

## Business Model Sketch

- **Outreach**: Contact authors directly (likely nonfiction / self-help / "life playbook" style books first, given the tone of the note)
- **Build**: Jay builds the website + app/agent layer on the author's behalf
- **Deal structure**: Request 40% ownership of the resulting product/venture in exchange for building and likely operating it
- **Content generation**: Use AI video generation (LTX mentioned) to create promotional or educational videos/movies based on book content
