# RoofClaim Recovery — Business Plan Notes

Raw notes (Apple Notes, captured with contact "Tony") outlining a business venture concept: a public-adjusting + AI-powered claim recovery company targeting residential roofing insurance claims.

## Concept

**RoofClaim Recovery** helps homeowners and roofing companies recover more money from insurance carriers on denied, delayed, or underpaid roof claims, faster and with less administrative overhead.

> "You fix the roof. We fight for the claim."

### Problem
Roof claims are messy — homeowners don't understand policies, roofers aren't insurance-claim experts, documentation is inconsistent, and carriers frequently deny, delay, or underpay.

### Solution / Claim Lifecycle
Inspect → Document → Analyze Policy → Build Scope → Submit/Supplement → Negotiate → Recover

Over time, two internal systems automate and improve this process:
- **RecoveryOS** — operational scale / workflow automation
- **Recovery Intelligence** — learns which evidence, strategies, and workflows produce better outcomes; becomes the long-term data moat

Stated framing: **Service → Software → Intelligence** — this progression is what could make the company larger than a traditional public adjusting (PA) firm.

## Customers & Monetization

Two channels:
1. **Homeowners** — company represents them, earns a percentage of recovered claim proceeds (where permitted).
2. **Roofing companies** — referral/strategic partners; help resolve their customers' claims and reduce roofers' claim-admin burden. Roofing companies were noted as planning to hand over their "problem files" (denied/underpaid claims) to route into the recovery pipeline.

### Draft Pricing / Fee Structure (rough, from notes — not finalized)
- PA firm flat rate: **$300** (billing support, paperwork)
- Appraisal request: triggered after a claim is denied or underpaid
- RoofClaim negotiation (flat-rate estimate, total scope): **$150** flat — covers writing supplement estimate/revision, adding/removing bid items
- Billing & claim negotiation: **10%** of total support of supplements (billing, support, bid items, invoices — handling non-"problem" files)
- Supplemental increase fee: **5%** of the supplemental increase, up to a certain cap (cap amount not specified)
- Problem-file / denied-claim flow (PA support): send email to adjuster → roofer estimate → possible second denial → appraisal → completed → results sent to roofing company, insured, and homeowner
- Guaranteed refund model mentioned for problem-file flat rate ($300)
- Appraisal company (3rd party): flat rate, amount unspecified
- Homeowner-facing feature: real-time updates on claim/appraiser status

> ⚠️ Note: pricing structure in the raw source is fragmented/inconsistent (multiple overlapping flat-rate and percentage figures for what may be the same service). Flagged here as raw and unreconciled — treat as draft notes, not a finalized pricing model.

## Go-to-Market Strategy

Prioritize **roofing company partnerships** before homeowner demand generation — one productive roofer partner can continuously feed claims, whereas acquiring homeowners individually is far more expensive.

Playbook:
1. Start with 20–50 local roofing companies
2. Offer free claim reviews
3. Demonstrate recoveries → build testimonials/case studies
4. Build referral engine
5. Expand geographically

Homeowner acquisition: dominate high-intent search terms ("roof claim denied", "insurance underpaid roof claim", "roof insurance supplement", storm-specific searches) via SEO, Google Local, educational content, post-storm direct mail, and retargeting.
