# Workday Agent Platform SEM Role — Opportunity Notes

Personal note captured re: a Senior Engineering Manager opening leading Workday's **Agent Platform** team (see [entity page](../entities/workday-agent-platform.md) for the technical/org details).

## Why It's Interesting

- Small, senior, high-leverage team working at "the front edge of a domain that is still being invented — where frameworks turn over in months and the durable bets are not obvious."
- The role is explicitly framed as a **judgment** job, not a coding job: "the hardest part of this job is not the code — it is choosing what to build, and what will still matter once today's tools are gone."
- Charter ownership: deciding build vs. adopt vs. partner across memory, identity, discovery, evaluation, and MCP/tool interfaces.
- Emphasis on turning agent run data (reasoning + tool-call trajectories) into a compounding feedback loop — a thesis I find compelling and aligned with how I think about agent evaluation and self-improvement loops.

## Role Requirements (raw capture)

- Track record leading platform/infra teams at scale (managing senior ICs and/or managers).
- **Hands-on** depth in agentic systems in production — runtimes/harnesses, tool use, memory, evaluation — "not oversight from a distance."
- Breadth across enterprise + internal use cases (dev tooling, ops, knowledge work) or consumer scale.
- Strong distributed systems / cloud / ML infra foundation.
- History of owning a platform charter and defending scope against pressure to "do everything," including build-vs-partner calls.

## Open Questions / Things to Probe in Interviews

- What does "member scale" actually mean in Workday's context (customer orgs vs. end users)?
- How mature is the current eval-gate / safe-rollout pipeline — is this greenfield or already load-bearing?
- Team size, reporting line within AI Platform, and how much cross-org political weight the role carries vs. IC-level technical authority.
