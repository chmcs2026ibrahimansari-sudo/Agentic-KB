# Reshma Sriraman — Warning Signs Your Company Is Stuck in AI Pilot Mode

## TL;DR
The post argues that enterprise AI stalls when demos and pilots never become governed, integrated, outcome-owned production workflows; because it is also a LuMay marketing post, treat it as a low-confidence market signal rather than neutral evidence.

## Key Points
- The post distinguishes AI ambition from AI production value: roadmaps, pilots, vendor demos, sponsorship, and budget do not matter if every conversation ends with "explore further."
- Seven pilot-mode warning signs are listed: each use case starts from zero; every pilot triggers a new architecture debate; security comes after the demo; integrations slow everything down; point solutions are disconnected; humans still do handoff work; ROI is measured after deployment instead of designed up front.
- Security/governance requirements named in the source include RBAC, auditability, guardrails, PII protection, and human oversight.
- The integration gap is framed as the difference between answering questions and doing work inside Salesforce, SQL, ServiceNow, CRMs, and ERPs.
- The post's outcome metrics are business-level: quality, cost, speed, revenue, customer experience, and risk reduction.
- LuMay is positioned as a unified foundation across interface, intelligence, orchestration, integration, and governance that can power multiple agent types.
- Comments repeatedly echo the same operating-model point: pilots fail to compound when nobody owns the platform decision and humans continue routing tasks, updating systems, or triggering approvals manually.
