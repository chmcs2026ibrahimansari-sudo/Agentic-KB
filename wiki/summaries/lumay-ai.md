# LuMay AI — Security-First Enterprise Agent Factory

## Source

- Raw source: `raw/framework-docs/lumay-ai.md`
- URL: https://www.lumay.ai/
- Captured context: Jay flagged this company/site alongside enterprise AI adoption notes to inspect for operating-model and governance patterns.

## TL;DR

LuMay AI markets a governed enterprise agent platform for regulated workflows, with security, approvals, auditability, human-in-the-loop controls, integrations, and outcome analytics positioned as platform foundations rather than add-ons.

## Key Points

- **Target market:** Regulated and operationally complex industries: legal, healthcare, financial services, technology, manufacturing, and enterprise operations.
- **Platform claim:** LuMay positions itself as one platform to build, govern, and scale AI agents across workflows, systems, data, APIs, and business functions.
- **Governed execution layer:** The site explicitly foregrounds approvals, monitoring, auditability, RBAC/access controls, and human review as core platform capabilities.
- **Six-layer architecture:** Interface → Intelligence → Orchestration → Integration → Governance → Analytics. Governance and analytics are first-class layers, not post-hoc reporting.
- **Product/service split:** The company offers both packaged agent categories (Voice, Legal, Workflow Automation, Insight, Forecasting, QMS/Compliance) and done-for-you AI engineering services for RAG, system integration, and SaaS AI features.
- **Self-reported outcomes:** The site claims 85% translation cost reduction, 4x faster support query resolution, and a five-day sketch-to-production AI teammate build. These remain vendor-reported.
