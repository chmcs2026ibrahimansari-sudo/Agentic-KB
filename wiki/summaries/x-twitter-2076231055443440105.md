## TL;DR

Jason Calacanis describes a prototype called PODMEME: a personalized podcast player that finds top tech/business podcast topics and lets the user play a cross-podcast stream by topic.

## Key Points

- The prototype finds common topics discussed across tech and business podcasts.
- It lets the user play a single stream across multiple podcasts by selected topic, using deep links into podcast segments.
- The example topic in the tweet is "China and open source."
- The prototype reportedly used Grok 4.5 and Perplexity Computer, costing 1,100 Perplexity credits, represented in the tweet as $11.
- The author is considering producing custom MP4 files for each topic with audio transitions.
